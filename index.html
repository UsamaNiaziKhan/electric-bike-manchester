UK Road-Legal E-Bikes: What the Law Says

In the UK, an electrically assisted pedal cycle (EAPC) is road legal without a licence, tax or insurance if the motor is rated 250W or under, assistance cuts out at 15.5 mph and the bike has working pedals. Every road-legal model at Manchester eBike follows these rules, so you can ride on roads and cycle paths across Manchester with no paperwork. Anything more powerful is classed as a moped and needs registration, which is why sticking to compliant bikes matters.

What to Check Before You Buy

Start with the battery: a 36V 10Ah pack suits short commutes, while long-range riders should look at 48V or 52V systems with higher amp-hour ratings. Next, consider the frame style. Folding frames suit flats and trains, step-through frames make hopping on and off easy, and fat tyres add grip and comfort on wet Manchester roads. Finally, check after-sales support.
